Cultural Heritage

The Land of Seven Languages

Kasaragod is uniquely known as 'Sapthabhasha Sangamabhoomi' (The land of seven languages). Due to its bordering geography, you can hear a harmonious blend of Malayalam, Tulu, Kannada, Marathi, Konkani, Beary, and Urdu here.

Theyyam Rituals

Theyyam is a vibrant and ancient ritualistic art form prominent in North Malabar. Performers don spectacular costumes and elaborate face painting, embodying divine deities. It bridges the gap between the divine and humanity through powerful dance.

Yakshagana

A traditional theater form that combines dance, music, dialogue, and extravagant costumes. Highly popular in the Tulu-speaking regions of Kasaragod, it narrates epic stories from the Ramayana, Mahabharata, and Puranas.
