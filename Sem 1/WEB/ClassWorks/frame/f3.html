Handlooms & Geography

Kasaragod Saree

Holding a Geographical Indication (GI) tag, the Kasaragod Saree is a traditional cotton handloom renowned for its high quality, durable thread work, and distinct traditional borders. It is a testament to the local weavers' enduring craftsmanship.

Pristine Coastlines & Rivers

The district is bordered by the majestic Western Ghats on the east and the Arabian Sea on the west. It is blessed with 12 rivers, including the Chandragiri and Tejaswini, weaving through lush landscapes, creating beautiful backwaters and golden beaches.

Coir Industry

Abundant in coconut groves, Kasaragod sustains a thriving traditional coir industry. The golden fiber extracted from coconut husks is woven into mats, ropes, and crafts, supporting the livelihood of local rural communities.
