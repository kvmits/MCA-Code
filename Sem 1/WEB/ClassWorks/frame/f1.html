Tourist Attractions

Bekal Fort

The largest fort in Kerala, Bekal Fort is a spectacular keyhole-shaped historical monument situated right by the Arabian Sea. It offers breathtaking views and a glimpse into the region's rich military past, standing strong for over 300 years.

Ranipuram

Often referred to as the "Ooty of Kerala," Ranipuram is a stunning hill station nestled in the Western Ghats. Its lush green shola woods, monsoon forests, and diverse wildlife make it a paradise for trekkers and nature lovers.

Ananthapura Lake Temple

The only lake temple in Kerala, believed to be the original seat (Moolasthanam) of Ananthapadmanabha Swami. The temple was historically guarded by a legendary, vegetarian crocodile named Babiya, adding to the mystical aura of the sacred shrine.
